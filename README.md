## 👨‍💼 About Me

I am a **Senior Business Analyst at Zencore Solutions Limited**, working at the intersection of business operations, technology and enterprise resource planning (ERP).

My work focuses on understanding business requirements, mapping business processes and translating operational needs into practical Odoo ERP solutions. I collaborate with stakeholders, technical teams and end users throughout the implementation lifecycle, helping businesses improve process visibility, efficiency and system adoption.

- 🏢 Working on Odoo ERP implementation and business process improvement.
- 🤝 Connecting business stakeholders with technical implementation teams.
- 📋 Translating business needs into clear requirements and functional workflows.
- 🧩 Focusing on Odoo functional workflows and business requirements.
- 💻 My technical learning background includes MERN stack development and Next.js.

## 🔄 My ERP Implementation Approach

1. **Discovery**  
   Understand business goals, pain points and operational challenges.

2. **Requirements Analysis**  
   Gather, organize and document business and functional requirements.

3. **Process Mapping**  
   Analyze current workflows and define improved future processes.

4. **Solution Design**  
   Align requirements with suitable Odoo ERP workflows and modules.

5. **Validation & Training**  
   Review solution flows, support UAT and help users adapt.

6. **Go-Live & Improvement**  
   Support deployment, adoption and continuous business improvement.
